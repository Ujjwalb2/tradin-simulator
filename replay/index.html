Gold Replay

Replay

  • Pick a start with Go (date and time in the chart timezone), 🎲 Random, or ⇤ Select bar and click a bar.
  • Step reveals one chart bar, or five minutes when set to Step: 5 min (watch a 1H or 4H candle form). Play keeps stepping at the chosen speed.
  • Switch timeframe at any time: the last bar on 15m, 1H, 4H and D is the one still forming, built from the 5-minute bars seen so far. The replay candle stays at the right edge with your zoom, and the price axis re-fits.
  • Scrolled back into history? » (bottom right of the chart) brings you back to the replay candle. Alt/⌥+R does the same and resets the zoom.
  • Daily bars run 17:00 → 17:00 New York, and 4H bars start at 17:00, 21:00, 01:00, 05:00, 09:00, 13:00 New York (the MT4/MT5 and TradingView convention).

Trading

  • Market orders fill at the close of the current 5-minute bar: buys at the ask, sells at the bid.
  • Limit and stop orders trigger on the 5-minute ask (buys) or bid (sells). A gap fills at the bar's open.
  • Longs exit on the bid and shorts on the ask, so every trade pays the real spread.
  • If a stop loss and take profit are both touched inside one 5-minute bar, the stop is assumed to hit first. On the bar a pending order fills, only the stop loss is checked.
  • ⌖ next to a field, then click the chart, sets that price. Risk % sizes the lot from your stop distance.
  • Going back in time needs no open positions; pending orders are cancelled. Going forward with Go plays every bar in between, so open trades are managed.

Drawing and indicators

  • Tools on the left: trend line, ray, horizontal and vertical line, parallel channel, zone, Fib retracement, long and short position, date and price range. The hint at the top says what to click.
  • A long/short position is three clicks (entry, stop, target) and plays out as you replay: it shows open, target hit or stopped in R, using the same fill rules as real orders.
  • Click a drawing to select it. Drag a handle to reshape it, drag anywhere else on it to move it. The bar above the chart sets colour, width and style, and has settings, clone, lock and delete.
  • Double-click a drawing (or right-click → Settings) to type exact prices and times. A position's box can be given an end time: drag its right-hand handle.
  • Drawings are pinned to time and price, so they stay in place on every timeframe.
  • EMAs: ƒx EMA adds one. Click an EMA's name in the chart legend (hover shows 👁 ⚙ ✕), double-click its line, or click EMA in the side panel to set length, source, colour, width, style. Changes show live; Cancel undoes them.
  • Fib settings (double-click it): turn levels on/off, edit their values and colours, add or remove levels, extend lines, show levels/prices/background, reverse.
  • Switching timeframe keeps the replay candle on screen at the same zoom, like TradingView replay. Scroll left to load older bars.

Tickers

  • Switch ticker with the menu at the top left (XAUUSD, NIFTY …). Each ticker keeps its own replay time, trades, drawings and settings.
  • NSE data has no bid/ask, so buys and sells fill at the last price; add slippage in Settings. The lot size (NIFTY 65) is editable there too.
  • Add another NSE index or stock with python src/build_nse.py --ticker BANKNIFTY (or RELIANCE, FINNIFTY …) and reload.

Keys

  • → step · Space play/pause · 1–5 timeframes · Esc cancel a tool or deselect
  • Del delete the selected drawing · ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z undo · ⇧⌘Z / Ctrl+Y redo

Saving

  • Everything (replay time, trades, drawings, settings) is saved in this browser automatically. Export CSV downloads the trade history.
